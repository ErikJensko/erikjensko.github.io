About Me

Aside from my physics and maths, my hobbies include music, sports, philosophy, travelling and literature. I especially enjoy playing piano works from composers such as Chopin, Liszt, Rachmaninoff, Debussy and Scriabin. I've recently been interested in phenomenological and existential philosophy, from Brentano and Husserl to Sartre and Camus.

I'm a powerlifter and and currently a member of UCL Barbell. I've previously competed at the British Powerlifting University Championships for the University of Nottingham, where we placed 4th at the IPF World University Powerlifting Cup (2018).

Slightly more related to my academic studies, I enjoy reading and debating topics in philosophy including metaphysics and the philosophy of science. In particular, I'm interested in the philosophical aspects of General Relativity, the cosmological constant and quantum gravity (e.g. see here for some workshops I've recently taken part in).

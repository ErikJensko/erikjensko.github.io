Research experience and education

EPSRC Doctoral Prize Research Fellow 2024–2026

Department of Mathematics, University College London.
Fellowship held from April 2024 to October 2026, including a six-month extension.

PhD in Mathematics 2019–2023

University College London. Awarded December 2023.
Thesis: A unified approach to geometric modifications of gravity.
Supervisor: Prof. Christian Böhmer.

MSc in Gravity, Particles and Fields 2017–2018

University of Nottingham, Distinction.
Thesis: Scalar fields in cosmology: a dynamical systems approach.
Supervisor: Prof. Edmund Copeland.

BSc in Physics with Theoretical Physics 2014–2017

University of Nottingham.
Dissertation: Vacuum instability of the Standard Model of Particle Physics.
Supervisor: Dr. Peter Millington.

Before my PhD, I worked as a Senior Algorithm Engineer at MBDA in Bristol (2018–2019), designing algorithms and carrying out statistical analyses in Python.

Research visits and collaborations

Research visits have been an important part of my work, particularly in developing collaborations on interacting dark sectors and geometric theories of gravity.

  • University of Science and Technology of China, November–December 2026 (planned). Invited visit hosted by Prof. Yi-Fu Cai, working on extended dark matter models.
  • University of Wrocław, September 2026. COST Short Term Scientific Mission on thermodynamic dark sector models and phase-space structures.
  • Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, June 2026. Research visit supported by a UCL MAPS ECR Travel Grant, on interacting dark sector models and elastic scattering.
  • University of Montpellier, November 2025. Collaboration with Dr. Vivian Poulin on entropy coupled interacting dark sector models.
  • Universidad de Salamanca, October 2025. Collaboration with Prof. Jose Beltrán Jiménez on effective field theory constructions and perturbative stability.
  • SISSA, June 2025. Research visit hosted by Prof. Enrico Barausse, on k-essence and astrophysical screening mechanisms.
  • Babeș-Bolyai University, October 2024. Collaboration with Prof. Tiberiu Harko on geometric modifications of gravity and cosmological constraints.
  • University of Science and Technology of China, April–May 2023. Research with Prof. Yi-Fu Cai on effective field theory approaches to strong coupling in modified gravity.
  • University of Tartu, August–October 2021. Research with Prof. Tomi Koivisto on Lagrangian formulations of relativistic interacting fluids.

Teaching and supervision

At UCL, I have delivered lectures for Mathematics for General Relativity (2023 and 2024), worked as a Senior Postgraduate Teaching Assistant for Computing for Mathematical Physics (2022 and 2023), and taught on An Introduction to Mathematica (2021). I also completed the UCL Arena Teaching Development Programme in 2021–2022.

I co-supervised a third-year dissertation on gravitational waves in f(G) gravity in 2025–2026, and a MAPS summer research project on Lie groups in theoretical physics in 2024.

Selected funding and awards

  • UCL Global Engagement Funds, co-applicant (£3,900), 2026: collaboration with the University of Tuzla on mathematical modelling and cosmology.
  • EPSRC Doctoral Prize Fellowship, 2024–2026, with a six-month extension in 2026.
  • IOP Publishing Impact Award, 2025, for work on spatial curvature in f(Q) cosmology.
  • International Visiting Scholar & Remote Collaboration Grant, USTC, 2023.
  • David Warren Fund Scholarship, UCL, 2022.

My research visits have also been supported by COST STSM grants, UCL MAPS, the Institute of Physics, the London Mathematical Society and the Dora Plus programme.

Service and outreach

I have mentored through In2Research, contributed to the UCL Mathematics EDI committee, and taken part in the Blackett Lab Family's Representing Physics event. I also organise a philosophy and space-time reading group at UCL.

I review for Classical and Quantum Gravity, Nuclear Physics B, JCAP, Journal of Mathematical Physics and Physics Letters B.
